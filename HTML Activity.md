Short description of tapsilog (follow this format):
Tapsilog is a popular Filipino breakfast meal made with tapa
(marinated beef), sinangag (garlic fried rice), and itlog (fried egg).
It is a simple yet flavorful dish commonly served with vinegar, atchara,
or other condiments.
Ingredients of Tapsilog
For the Tapa:
• 500 g beef slices
• 4 cloves garlic, minced
• 3 tablespoons soy sauce
• 2 tablespoons vinegar
• 2 tablespoons brown sugar
• ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
• 1 tablespoon cooking oil
For the Sinangag (Garlic Rice):
• 3 cups cooked rice
• 4 cloves garlic, minced
• 1 tablespoon cooking oil
• Salt to taste
For the Itlog (Fried Egg):
• 2–3 eggs
• Cooking oil
• Salt and pepper to taste
Serving Suggestion
Serve tapsilog hot and freshly cooked on a plate with:
• Garlic fried rice (sinangag)
• Fried or sunny-side-up egg (itlog)
• Marinated beef tapa
• Sliced fresh tomatoes or cucumber
• Atchara (pickled papaya) as a side
• A small dish of spiced vinegar or soy sauce for dipping

For presentation, place the garlic rice on one side, tapa in the center,
and the fried egg beside the rice. Garnish with sliced tomatoes or
spring onions for a fresh touch.
